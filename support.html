Common questions

How do I scan a page? Open the app, point the camera at printed text and hold steady. The app captures the page automatically. You can also choose a photo from your library.

Does it work offline? Yes. Most definitions come from dictionaries bundled in the app. Only words not found there are looked up online.

What data do you collect? None. See the Privacy Policy.
