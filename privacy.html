PagePeek Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026

PagePeek does not collect, store, or share personal information. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking. This page explains what the app does with the things it touches.

Camera and photos

The app uses your camera to scan pages of text, and lets you pick a photo from your library instead. The text in the image is recognized on your device using Apple's Vision framework. Images are not uploaded anywhere, and the app does not keep them after you close a page. Choosing a photo uses Apple's system photo picker, so the app never gets access to your whole photo library.

Motion sensors

The app reads the motion sensors only to tell when the phone is steady enough to capture a page. This data stays on your device and is not recorded.

Definition lookups

Most definitions come from dictionaries bundled inside the app and work offline. When a word isn't found there, the app may look it up online, sending only that single word (not the image, not the surrounding text) to these services:

  • Wiktionary (en.wiktionary.org), operated by the Wikimedia Foundation
  • Free Dictionary API (api.dictionaryapi.dev)

As with any web request, these services can see your device's IP address and handle it under their own privacy policies. The app attaches no account, device identifier, or other information to these requests.

Web search

If you tap the option to search the web for a word, the app opens Google in your browser with that word as the search. Anything that happens after that is governed by Google's policies.

Settings stored on your device

Your preferences, such as highlight style, minimum word length, and enabled languages, are saved locally on your device and are never transmitted.

Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes
